Terms of use

Last updated: {updated}

These rules apply to members who use the member page of this website.

  • The files on the member page (sheet music, recordings and lyrics) are for members of the orchestra, to rehearse and perform with the orchestra only.
  • Do not share these files, publish them, copy them for other people or put them on other websites.
  • Your account is personal. Do not share it or your password with anyone.
  • Admins only put files on the website that the orchestra is allowed to use.
  • The orchestra may remove your access or any file at any time.

Questions about these rules? Write to {cdi:site.foundation.email}.
