1. What is this website?

The orchestra is really two groups in one: a band and a large choir. The website does two jobs:

🌍 A window to the outside

Anyone can read about the orchestra, see when it performs, and find out how to join. No account needed.

🔒 A private place for members

Members log in to download sheet music, play recordings and get lyrics. A few members, the admins, keep it all up to date themselves, without needing a programmer.

2. Who uses it?

There are four kinds of visitors. Each one can do a bit more than the one before.

👀 Visitor

Not logged in

Reads the public pages and can send a message through the contact form.

🎵 Member

Has an account

Everything a visitor can do, plus the member page with music, and their own account page.

🛠️ Admin

Trusted member

Everything a member can do, plus managing songs, page texts, images and people.

👑 Master

One account

Everything an admin can do, plus removing admins. This is the "owner" account.

Good to know: there is no public sign-up. Nobody can create an account for themselves. An admin always adds new people, so only real members get in.

Public pages (anyone can open these)

🏠 About Public (this is the home page)

The first page people see. It tells the story of the orchestra: who you are, the band and the choir, the conductor, and when and where rehearsals take place.

  • Read the text and look at photos
  • Admins can rewrite the whole text and swap the photos themselves

✉️ Become a member Public

For people who would like to join the choir. It has three parts:

  • A general text about joining
  • An audition section (dates, how to sign up). When there are no auditions, admins empty the text and the whole section disappears
  • A contact form: the visitor fills in their email address, a subject and a message. The message is sent by email to all admins and the master. The website does not keep the message. The admin simply replies to the email

📅 Agenda Public

One free text with the upcoming performances: dates, times and places. Admins write it in their own words. When it is empty, the page says "There are no performances planned at the moment."

🔑 Log in Public

Members type their email address and password. If something is wrong, the website only says "Email address or password is incorrect". It never says which of the two, so strangers cannot find out who has an account.

  • A button to show or hide the password while typing
  • A "Forgot password?" link
  • If you tried to open a members page before logging in, you land on that page after logging in

📨 Forgot password, and Choose a new password Public

You type your email address and get an email with a one-time link. The link works for 1 hour and only once. On the page that opens, you choose a new password (at least 12 characters, not a very common one).

📜 Privacy, Cookies, Terms of use Public

Short, clear pages in the footer: what personal data the website uses and for how long, which (single) cookie is used, and the rules for the music files (members only, please don't share).

Pages for logged-in people

🎼 Member page Members

A list of all songs the orchestra is working on right now. Every song shows its files, grouped:

  • Sheet music (PDF), with clear names like "Soprano" or "Trumpet 1". Everyone sees every part
  • Audio (MP3): play it right on the page, or download it
  • Lyrics (text file): download only

It works well on a phone. A short reminder says the files are for members only. Old songs that have been archived are not shown to members.

👤 My account Members

Shows your own email address and role. Here you can change your own password: type the current one, then the new one twice. Afterwards you stay logged in on this device, you are logged out everywhere else, and you get an email telling you the password was changed (in case it wasn't you).

Pages for admins

🎶 Song management Admins

  • Add a song (it is visible to members straight away)
  • Edit the title and notes
  • Add files (PDF, MP3 or text, up to 15 MB each), rename them or remove them
  • Archive a song: members no longer see it, but nothing is lost and you can bring it back with one click
  • Delete a song for good (only after a confirmation that names the song)

✏️ Edit pages Admins

A list of the four texts admins can change: About, Become a member, Audition and Agenda, each with the date it was last changed. Opening one shows an editor that works a bit like a word processor:

  • Headings, bold, italic, lists, links, simple tables, quotes and text alignment
  • Add pictures, choose their size and position, and always give them a short description for people who cannot see them
  • A preview shows exactly how it will look before you save
  • After saving, the change appears on the website within about a minute
Careful: the website does not remember older versions of a text. Copy the old text somewhere before rewriting a big one.

🖼️ Image library Admins

All uploaded pictures in one place, with their descriptions. Upload new ones (JPEG, PNG or WebP), see which ones are not used anywhere, and delete the ones you no longer need. Deleting asks you to confirm and shows where the picture is still used. A deleted picture cannot be restored. Only use photos of people who agreed to it.

👥 User management Admins

  • Add a person by typing just an email address. They automatically get an email to choose their own password. Admins never see or know anyone's password
  • See an overview of everyone: role, date added, last login, and when the last email was sent. "Never logged in" means the person has not chosen a password yet
  • Send the email again if it got lost
  • Remove a member who leaves (they lose access immediately)
  • Make a member an admin

Only the master can also remove an admin or take admin rights away. That way admins cannot lock each other out. Nobody can remove or change the master from the website.

Other bits

  • Friendly error pages ("Page not found", "No access") with a short reference number that makes a problem easy to report.
  • Cookie notice: one short message, because the site only uses one cookie, to keep you logged in. There is no tracking.

4. How things work, step by step

🙋 A new member gets access

  1. An admin types the new member's email address.
  2. The website emails them a one-time link.
  3. They choose their own password.
  4. They log in and see the member page.

🔁 Someone forgets their password

  1. On the login page: "Forgot password?"
  2. They type their email address. The site always answers the same, whether or not an account exists.
  3. If there is an account, an email arrives with a link (valid 1 hour).
  4. They choose a new password and log in.

🎧 A member prepares a rehearsal

  1. They log in.
  2. On the member page they find the song.
  3. They play the recording on the page, and download their own part.
  4. The download link only works for a short time, and only for logged-in members.

➕ An admin adds a new song

  1. Open Song management, add the title.
  2. Add the files: parts, recordings, lyrics. Give each a clear name.
  3. Members can see it immediately.
  4. When the song is over, archive it. It disappears for members but stays safe.

📝 An admin updates the agenda

  1. Open Edit pages and choose Agenda.
  2. Change the text in the editor.
  3. Click Preview to check how it looks.
  4. Save. Within about a minute everyone sees the new text.

💬 Someone wants to join the choir

  1. A visitor reads Become a member.
  2. They fill in the contact form.
  3. The message is emailed to all admins (and the master).
  4. An admin replies from their own email. The website keeps no copy of the message.

👋 Someone leaves the orchestra

  1. An admin opens User management.
  2. Clicks Remove next to the name and confirms in the pop-up.
  3. The account is gone and access stops at once, even on a phone that is still logged in.

🔐 Logging in and out

  1. You log in with email and password.
  2. You stay logged in for at most 1 day, then you log in again.
  3. After several wrong passwords, the website makes you wait a little before trying again.
  4. Click Log out and you are fully out.

5. Behind the scenes

These are the "quality" promises of the website, explained simply.

🛡️ Safe

  • Passwords are never stored in readable form. They are scrambled in a way that cannot be reversed, with an extra secret ingredient that is kept outside the database.
  • Music files sit in a private store. Members only get a link that expires after a short time.
  • Every rule is checked on the website's side, so nobody can bypass it by being clever.
  • Admins cannot do things that only the master may do, even by trying.
  • Whenever something is deleted or removed, the website always asks you to confirm first, tells you exactly what will happen, and says whether it can be undone.
  • Lots of automatic security checks run before every update goes live.

🔎 Traceable

  • Every change an admin makes is written to a logbook: who did what, and when. For rights changes it records who changed whose rights.
  • The logbook (and normal logs) are kept for 30 days, then removed.
  • Passwords and secrets never end up in the logbook.

🙈 Private

  • Only the personal data that is really needed: an email address and a role.
  • No tracking, no advertising, nothing loaded from other websites.
  • Data is stored in the European Union (the Netherlands).
  • People can ask what is stored about them, or have it removed. This is answered within a month.
  • Photos of people are only used with their permission.

♿ For everyone

  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Can be used with just a keyboard, and works with screen readers.
  • Clear colours with good contrast, including a dark mode that follows your device.
  • Written in English, with all dates and times in Amsterdam time, written out clearly, like "Sunday 13 December 2026, 20:00".

⚡ Fast and cheap

  • Pages are light and load quickly, even on a mobile connection.
  • The website "sleeps" when nobody is using it, so you pay almost nothing when it is quiet. It only costs when people actually use it.
  • A limit on how much it can scale up protects against a surprise bill, and an alert warns about spending.
  • All tools used are free. The only cost you can't avoid is probably the domain name (about €10 a year).

🔧 Reliable

  • Many automatic tests check every feature before anything changes on the live website.
  • A new version is first started in the background and tested. Only if it passes does it replace the old one. If not, nobody notices anything.
  • Updates to the building blocks of the website happen automatically, with extra care for big ones.
  • If the website goes down, the owner gets an alert. Going back to the previous version takes one command.
  • No promise of 100% uptime. It is a volunteer orchestra's website, not a bank. Short interruptions can happen.

💾 Backups

  • The database (people, texts, song information) is backed up every week and kept for 30 days.
  • Music files that are removed by mistake can be restored for 7 days.
Important: the music files and pictures are not backed up automatically. Admins keep the originals of everything they upload, on their own computer or a shared drive.

🤝 Not dependent on one person

  • Everything is written down: how it works, how to fix problems, and step-by-step instructions for admins.
  • At least two owners have access to the online accounts, so the site does not depend on one person.
  • The whole setup can be rebuilt from the instructions if it ever has to be.

7. How it gets built

  1. Foundation. The workshop: the safety checks, the automatic tests, and a first empty page that goes live the right way.
  2. Accounts. Logging in, passwords, adding people, and "My account".
  3. Public site. The About, Become a member and Agenda pages, the editor for admins, and the legal pages.
  4. Music. Songs, files, and the member page with downloads and playing recordings.
  5. Launch. Final safety checks, a test of the backups, checking that emails arrive, filling in all the real content, and going live.

There is also a clickable demo (the demo folder): open index.html in a browser and click around as a visitor, member, admin or master. Nothing in it is real, it only shows how the pages and flows feel.

8. Small word list

Admin
A trusted member who can manage songs, texts, pictures and people.
Master
The one owner account. Like an admin, plus it can remove admins.
Archive
Hide a song from members without deleting anything. It can be brought back any time.
Preview
A look at how a text will appear on the website, before saving it.
Alt text
A short description of a picture, for people who cannot see it (for example someone using a screen reader).
Cookie
A tiny note the website leaves in your browser. This site only uses one, to remember that you are logged in.
Audit trail / logbook
The list of who changed what and when, kept for 30 days.
Backup
A spare copy of the data, in case something is lost.
Domain name
The address of the website, like youroffice.org.
Summary of the project documents in the docs folder. The detailed technical version is in docs/PLAN.md. Names, texts and details are placeholders until the orchestra provides the real ones.
